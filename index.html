RECASTResearch manuscript

See risk before
it becomes failure.

Forecasting trajectory-level safety risks in multi-turn conversations, with early warnings informed by how risks accumulate.

Explore RECAST ↗
Inside the method
Model the trajectory, then forecast the first failure.

RECAST combines local interaction windows with long-term memory, revisits evidence in the context of the current turn, and estimates first-failure risk within a future window.
